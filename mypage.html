About Me

David Ayobamidele Badejo is a dual-background professional bridging Architectural Design and IT Engineering. He runs his own design practice, DB ArchiTech Studio, and works at Briech Atlantic Limited in Abuja across both architecture and IT functions. David also teaches on the AQskill platform. He holds an HND in Architecture (graduating top of his class in 2024) and a B.Sc. in Information Technology, alongside cybersecurity certifications, a combination that lets him approach design problems with both a builder's eye and a technologist's mindset.
